# Authors and attribution

## wardrobe (previously Clipline)

**[@a2289866844](https://github.com/a2289866844)** develops and maintains this independent modified application. The wardrobe contributions include clipboard collection, full-content Copy controls, screenshot recovery, card interaction changes, performance work and the 1.2 archive/search integration. See [the contribution table](docs/CHANGES-FROM-TENDEDERO.md) for version boundaries and source evidence.

The included **ClipdCore** source and tests are contributed from the maintainer’s local Clipd project. They provide archive storage, Chinese text indexing, OCR and related capture primitives used by wardrobe. The publication includes the library and its tests, without the separate Clipd app, launcher or personal data.

## Upstream

**[Alejandro Buján](https://github.com/alejandrobujan)** created [Tendedero](https://github.com/alejandrobujan/tendedero), the original screenshot shelf and hanging-card concept on which wardrobe is based.
